## What changed

`experiments/autobench/abr/tools/b0_extract.py` replaced, in each family file, every definition whose source text was
byte-identical to the reference copy (fin-apintake, or mkt-reviews for `lib.canon`) with an alias to
`abr/lib/common.py` or `abr/lib/host.py`. 17 targets; 131 replacements across the 12 families; 311 lines removed net
(`b0-extract-report.json`). Kept as family variants (not replaced): `abr-hr-referral/harness.py:load_solver` (its copy
differs). `host.Ctx` is the abr-ops-slawatch copy, a superset of the other nine (`calls` and `fetch_many`, which those
families never read). Functions bound to module globals became factories bound at import (`make_trigger_seed(gen.FAMILY)`,
`make_stream_hashes(gen.sha)`). The gate skeleton itself (the per-gate logic) was not moved: the copies differ per family
(G-specific variants in 3-10 forms each), so moving them is B1/B2 work, not a byte-identical extraction.

**Why the falsifier is not done.** A family's full gate run (solvability on 14 streams, 15-23 controls on 5 streams, the
mutation gate) runs thousands of triggers through AutomationBench's own tool path: on the order of a CPU-hour per family
and copy, so about 24 CPU-hours for 12 families before and after. The shared machine ran at a load average of 25-35 on 4
cores during this session; four gate processes (nice 0 from 18:11Z) accrued about 3 CPU-minutes each in 55 minutes and
were stopped at 18:35Z. Nothing was concluded from the partial runs. To finish it on a quiet machine:

```sh
S=<scratch>; cp -r <tree at 78f90d08>/experiments/autobench $S/before; cp -r <this branch>/experiments/autobench $S/after
for f in $(ls $S/after/families | grep '^abr-' | grep -v -e itsm-queue -e p2p-audit -e reg-incident-clock); do
  for side in before after; do d=$S/$side/families/$f
    if [ -f $d/gates.py ]; then PY=$d/gates.py; else PY="$d/harness.py gates"; fi
    corpus/AutomationBench/.venv/bin/python -I -B $PY > $S/$side-$f.json; done
  cmp $S/before-$f.json $S/after-$f.json && echo "$f identical"; done
```
