## AutomationBench (Zapier v1.0.6)

**Our local figures are not leaderboard numbers.** Opus 5 max 50.3% and Sonnet 5 max 34.67% come from the README's
600-task **public-set** table. The official leaderboard scores a **held-out private set** that Zapier says is "purposely
harder". On that set Opus 5 max scores 26.94% and Sonnet 5 max 10.65%.

**Official private-set leaderboard (zapier.com/benchmarks), strict pass:**

| Rank | Model (effort) | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gemini 4 Argon (High) | **51.29%** |
| 3 | Sonnet 5.5, default fallbacks (Max) | 44.75% |
| 4 | Opus 5.5, default fallbacks (Max) | 42.47% |
| 5 | GPT-6 Astra (Max) | 41.40% |
| 11 | GPT-6 Sol (XHigh) | 33.20% |
| 23 | Opus 5 (Max) | 26.94% |
| 67 | **Haiku 5.5 (Max)** | **12.02%** (launch version; Zapier will re-evaluate) |
| 72 | Sonnet 5 (Max) | 10.65% |

- **Protocol:** at most 50 steps; deterministic final-state assertions; Zapier reports variance of about 1% or less.
- **AutomationBench-AA** (INDEPENDENT) is a different metric: partial credit on the 657-task held-out set. Argon 77.5%,
  Sonnet 5.5 71.8%.

**Rule for our docs.** Always state:
- the split (public 600 vs private);
- the version (1.0.6);
- the metric (strict pass vs AA partial credit);
- the fallback setting.

Our dev60 numbers (Haiku plus our harness 30–37% on public devTrain tasks) **must not** be compared with private-board
numbers.
