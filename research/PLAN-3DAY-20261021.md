Realistic at this pace: **about 2–3 more weeks** for the technical vision. That is weeks, not months.

## Gates that can stretch the plan

1. **The weekly usage limit.** Last week it hit on day 5. Mitigations:
   - Sonnet for fan-out research;
   - fewer, larger agents;
   - commit and resume points before every long run;
   - $0 work while paid runs proceed.
2. **Machine limits:** 4 CPUs and about 2 GB of free disk. Paid runs go one at a time per bed, with a disk check
   before each launch.
3. **The Mercury allowance** is unknown. Spend is capped at 5M tokens until the owner reports what is left.
4. **A refutation.** For example, SOP-Bench sealed below the agent, or SPEC-1 recovery below 0.6. The plan then
   re-routes, and a recorded refutation counts as a result.
