## 3. Reconciliation by mechanism

### Construction knowledge versus construction intelligence

**Problem:** infer an appropriate executable process rather than recognize a
named family. **Existing machinery:** Foundry has a useful corpus of supplied
architectures, motif checks and failure explanations. However, its respecting
provider copies supplied answer dictionaries or calls its own objective compiler
(`src/construction.mjs:343-406`). Objective expected outputs are generated by the
same composer (`src/construction-objective.mjs:509-544`). Default comparative
session records are metadata, not two executed model arms
(`src/construction.mjs:448-508`).

**Decision:** retain these as explicitly labeled baselines and mechanism examples.
Do not count their positives as architecture invention or a measured skills
treatment effect. A leakage-marker check does not make the oracle's closure
state independent, and feedback from a test exposes that test to later work.
**Discriminator:** two actually executed candidate processes against independent
consequences. The HTTP witness already demonstrates why this is necessary.
TypeSafe's public workflow evaluation likewise assumes a correct harness; that
assumption cannot discharge Foundry's synthesis obligation [E1].

### Typed versions, execution, and evidence binding

**Problem:** keep an approved program, its effects and its evidence from drifting
apart. **Implemented substrate:** Foundry has content-addressed workflow versions,
expected-head/next-version checks and transactional proposal application
(`src/store.mjs:54-90,279-321`); execution checks stored program, registry, engine
and event history (`src/runtime.mjs:138-190`); delivery checks exact
request/proposal/run linkage (`src/foundry.mjs:59-70`). Independent task
qualification is deliberately separate from local success.

**Decision:** keep and test these mechanisms. A graph's authoring flags are not
evidence that a human approved it, a sandbox exists, or an external effect occurred.
The independent `evaluateWorkflow` surface can execute native candidates and
check explicit outputs or simulator state (`src/evaluation.mjs:101-166`), unlike
the current construction interpreter. The host-provided oracle still needs review.
**Discriminator:** candidate passes structural checks but fails an independently
observed obligation. Test stale/mismatched evidence and skipped-branch outputs.
Temporal's command/history replay is relevant prior art for separating durable
execution from metadata, not evidence of Foundry parity [E2].

### Goal identity versus plan revision

**Problem:** replacing a method must not replace or complete the underlying goal.
**Implemented substrate:** AMP's `active_plan.rs:314-390` resolves a canonical
goal binding, checks predecessor heads and rejects a revision that switches the
logical goal or task. Its source root is
`/home/raed/Projects/amp-grok-cognition-20260915`, not the nearly empty
`/home/raed/Projects/hippocampus` directory. The daemon's
`crates/hippocampusd/src/task_api.rs:22-67` consumes typed task/checkpoint context
through the project authorization boundary. These are source-level consumers;
the running service was not exercised here.

**Decision:** extract the invariants and a minimal standalone representation,
not AMP's entire storage/runtime dependency. Typed bindings are valuable only if
the inferred bindings are correct; they do not solve natural-language intent by
themselves. **Discriminator:** a method-only amendment versus cancellation,
followed by a child completion and a restart. The parent goal must survive only
where the source actually retains it. LangGraph's explicit checkpoint/thread
selection is relevant state-continuation machinery, not an intention oracle [E3].

### Provenance, ambiguity and operator structure

**Problem:** identical present actions can have different future revocation or
clarification behavior. ARC's `scripts/intent-program-v1.mjs:1-39,86-131` preserves
an authoritative source distinction and source hashes/spans, but also uses a
bounded regex-based projection. Its declared topology covers a single action,
AND and total sequence; alternatives, conditionals and revisions can be refused.
It cannot be inherited as a general intent solution. Its quote masking also
deserves adopted-quotation counterexamples before reuse.

**Decision:** retain raw evidence, source-specific revisions and explicit
unsupported meaning. Expand behavior only with separation witnesses, not by
adding an ever larger cue taxonomy. AMP's revision/currentness machinery is a
candidate substrate; its full source and limits are covered in the companion
ARC/AMP audit. **Discriminator:** identical current restriction, different source;
withdraw only one source and observe whether the correct obligation survives.
W3C PROV-DM gives explicit derivation, revision, quotation and invalidation
relations; it does not decide what a user's words authorize [E4].

### Evidence-sensitive choice versus useful adaptation

**Problem:** evidence can change a model's answer without improving its choice.
The finalized Neural Core v3 study and independent audit are relevant here, but
are not a dependency of Foundry. The current session read the source critic and
audit, and checked all **101 manifest entries**: 97 artifact files and four source
files match. It did not load tensors or replay neural predictions/metrics.

The existing independent audit reports useful choices on the exposed panel, but
full qualification fails because all condition-ID fit ceilings miss their fixed
NRMSE threshold. Evidence-conditioned reselection on unseen observations also
fails. Therefore this is neither “no useful learning occurred” nor “the evidence
encoder alone caused the failure.” Source:
`/home/raed/AgenticShared/arc-mnc-lab-20260910/contrast-credit-v3-results-independent-audit-20260917/NOTE.md:7-22,79-110,139-162`.
The critic's matched reversal calculation is implemented at
`contrast-credit-qualification-v3-20260917/core.py:123-143`.

**Decision:** import the experimental discipline: same candidates, consequentially
different evidence, measure both correct choices and actual usefulness. Do not
import a claim of learned program invention. **Discriminator:** the paired Jev
study below, including no-evidence and exchanged-evidence controls.

## 4. Jev belongs throughout the process

Candidate uses include screening research relevance, distinguishing current from
quoted instructions, judging consequential ambiguity, checking pattern
applicability, examining outcome evidence and localizing a repair. A large model
can propose candidate meanings and architectures; Jev can judge bounded
questions; deterministic code can enforce exact relations; observations establish
what actually happened. This is an architectural hypothesis to test, not an
assertion that these roles are already validated.

Current TypeSafe documentation describes typed decisions against shared state
[E5]. Its confidence field is derived from the probability distribution, not an
independent witness [E6]. Parallel questions do not imply independent truth
events, and marginal confidence cannot replace coupled constraints or authority.

The handoff's successful Vercel Gateway smoke test was **not reproduced**. The
current COS process has neither checked gateway key variable. No secret was read
or printed and no live Jev/provider experiment was attempted. Historical access
in a different process is not proof that this process inherits it. The direct
TypeSafe endpoint must not silently replace the requested gateway.

`JEV_DIAGNOSTIC_PROTOCOL.md` makes the next study concrete: six evidence-changing
pairs across interpretation, revocation, pattern applicability, research,
outcomes and repair, with additional abstention cases. It specifies controls,
full probability retention, harmful-decision accounting, matched-pair scoring,
source/model/prompt receipts and limits on claims. Its cases and API integration
still need to be instantiated and frozen before a live run. There is no Jev
performance result in this recovery.

## 5. What changes next

The strongest immediate conclusion is about the **evaluation boundary**. Before
expanding the compiler or replacing its regexes with model labels, compare its
candidates against executable consequences and independent obligations. Merely
adding Jev to the old circular evaluator can make a defective system appear
more intelligent without addressing its missing semantics.

The branch-runtime discriminator is already executed and retained. Repairing
that particular join would be useful engineering, but would not demonstrate
unseen-objective synthesis. The next semantic experiment should ask whether a
bounded judge preserves a source distinction and thereby selects the correct
process under a controlled intervention. Follow that with independently authored
objectives whose architectural requirements are not named in their prompts.

The desired standalone design should borrow Foundry's execution/evidence
boundaries, AMP's goal/plan and continuation invariants, ARC's distinction between
protected intent and revisable projections, and Neural Core's choice-quality
controls. None of those imports, individually or together, proves that the
intended process has been identified or constructed.
