`termination.json` records the call start, deadline, abort time, primary observation, up to eight secondary observations, finish time and final caller-aborted state. A primary observation includes its kind, local observation time, selected code and original error details. `failure.json` repeats that primary observation. Pending gateway handlers are drained after cancellation so their partial-body and secondary-error receipts are collected before final receipt writing.

These fields describe **first observed cause in this adapter's execution order**. They are not an assertion about the earliest physical network event, remote-server cause, or an independently synchronized clock. A body error observed after a recorded deadline does not displace that deadline; a timer firing after an already observed network error does not turn the earlier error into a timeout. Local timestamps use `Date.now()` and are not independent attestations. Secondary observations are bounded, not an exhaustive history.
