# Hollin Outlet: returns and cancellations

*Applies to the Hollin Outlet marketplace store only (bol and Amazon listings).*

- Outlet customers may cancel up to 24 hours after placing, even after dispatch; the marketplace refunds them.
- No fraud review: the marketplace screens payments.
- Out-of-stock outlet items are cancelled automatically after 48 hours.
- Shipping is a flat 3.95 per order, whatever the number of parcels.
- Returns within 30 days, refund within 14 days of receiving the item.
