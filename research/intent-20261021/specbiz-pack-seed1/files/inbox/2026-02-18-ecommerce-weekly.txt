Subject: 5 quick wins for March

1. Retry declined cards after 24 hours: merchants recover up to 15% of failed payments.
2. Send a "we miss you" voucher to customers who cancel.
3. Ship before you charge to beat the carrier cutoff.
4. Ask for reviews three days after delivery.
5. Batch your notifications to save on e-mail costs.
