## Spec ambiguities resolved
- **Tap method.** I matched world-filled pool templates, never forked the renderer. Frames are pinned to the bed pools via `framesFromPool`. A fact with no span is 'missing', one with two or more spans is 'ambiguous', and both are dropped.
- **Train bucket.** The skeleton-bucket holdout hashes the masked skeleton of the label-bearing span. Train, calib-in, dev-select and test-in read bucket 'train' (40 to 100%). calib-ood reads 30 to 40%, dev-ood 20 to 30%, and test-ood 0 to 20%.
- **Held-out families.** X5, X1 and X7 kinetics are test-ood only, and their spans are registered via `registerHeldout`/`heldoutSpans`. The held-out check runs before any bed import, so it fails closed.
- **Held-out injection text.** Rows carrying held-out injection text outside test-ood are dropped (this affected the K3 preamble and referenced header).
- **X5 registration.** Only unit-bearing lines and real SOP heads are registered, not unit-less headers.
- **Wave logic.** X3 is wave 1 only if the tap passes 95% (`tapStatus`, `opts.x3Wave`). Smoke ports use a deterministic world subsample: K1 every 3rd world, X4 every 8th, K2 and K3 every 12th.
- **Pairs and twins.**
  - Polarity pairs use variant 'flip' with a `pairId`; X8 flip twins change only the unit statement.
  - X3 invariance twins swap the author only.
  - Presence-matched unknowables replace the unit statement with a same-register line.
  - Paraphrase-consistency records live in test-ood.
- **Duplicate register rows.** K2 drops duplicate register rows, and the aliases resolve only against unique descriptions.
- **X3 label weights.** I used rare-label-weighted draws for X3, which brought the ratio from 3.8 to 2.4.
- **Wordings.** Slice wordings are supplied via `opts.wordings`. The slice table was measured with stub wordings, not owner-authored ones.

## Interface notes

**train-pools**
- The generator reads `<dir>/<port>.jsonl` through `opts.pools = {dir?, digest}`. `dir` defaults to `ctx.poolsDir`, and the digest is checked (error code `POOL_DIGEST`).
- It consumes these groups:
  - `paraphrase`.
  - `counterfactual`: year-first lines labelled `ymd`, restamped-without-window, reliable-with-window, and the KOBO kinds.
  - `authored`: X5 `assumption` lines with `assertsPort`, and X1 `positive`, `benign`, `decoy` and `hard-negative` lines.
- The real pools in `.foundry/kev-data/pools/train` work with 0 check errors and no shortfall.
- The pools have no carrier lines, so pass `builtin: ['carriers']`. Without it every injection job comes up short.

**heldout-author, through assembly**
- OOD lines are passed in `opts.lines`, each with `slice` set to `calib-ood`, `dev-ood` or `test-ood`.
- Line kinds the generator uses:
  - Labelled R-port lines with the label of the option.
  - `kind: 'nondeciding'` with `label: null`. Its `{S}` is filled with the other system.
  - `group: 'carrier'` with `label` set to the wrong option.
  - X5 `assumption` lines with `assertsPort` and `label`.
  - X1 `positive`, `benign`, `decoy` and `hard-negative` lines with `label` `yes` or `no`.
  - R4 lines may set `windowed`.
- The wordings go in `opts.wordings = {port: [{id, text}]}` and the cities in `opts.cities`. Pass `dev-ood`'s `ymd` lines for BRIGHTDESK, ATLAS and VENDEX, or R1 `ymd` is not offered in OOD.

**assembly**
- The generator file is `ports/records-notes.mjs`. Default targets come from `DEFAULT_ROWS` and `TEST_OOD_AXES`; `opts.targets`, `opts.scale`, `opts.ports` and `opts.control` override them.
- The span registry needs the held-out bed templates, and the held-out author never sees bed templates. Use `await heldOutBedSpans(beds)`, which returns 42 skeletons (R1 6, R2 16, R3 8, R4 12). Register each entry like this:
  `registry.add(e.text, {slice: 'heldout', source: 'bed-template', port: e.port, names: e.masks.names, markers: e.masks.markers})`.
- To check your own lines, call `makeSolverContext(bed, lines, {cities})` with the same lines and cities the build used. The test passes `builtinLines(bed, true)` plus the stub lines, and `gen.loadBed(beds)` supplies the bed.
- The stub fixtures in `test/records.test.mjs` show every shape above.
